Learn more about our school, location and educational environment.

Welcome to Iyunga

Iyunga Secondary School is a government secondary school located in Mbeya City, Tanzania.

The school provides secondary education while also emphasizing technical and practical skills that prepare students for further education, employment and self-reliance.

Our goal is to provide an environment where students can develop academically, socially, morally and practically.
